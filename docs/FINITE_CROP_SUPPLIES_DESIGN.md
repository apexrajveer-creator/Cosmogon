## Mechanism and honest scope

Add separately pinned optional supply-study configuration, independent of original version1/version2 crop definitions and existing exact forecast output. Explicit user-assumed budgets: liquid water kg, CO2 kg, HNO3-equivalent nutrient kg, lighting electrical energy J. No salt recipe, tank acid, nutrient/purification hardware, cooling/pumps/dehumidification energy or calorie adequacy implied.

At each forecast age, compute additional material-recipe cumulative demands from the exact separate edible/inedible dry production. Liquid demand includes metabolic incorporation plus independently retained optional light-period transpiration; transpiration remains vapor transfer with no collection credit. Water disabled/legacy cannot supply total liquid demand: unavailable instead of using metabolic water alone as a misleading total. Lighting demand uses the original actual assumed fixture power/16-hour photoperiod integral and excludes other facility energy. Material-recipe oxygen is projected external output, separate from the MEC reference O2 quantity, never a finite-stock grant.

Evaluate each finite budget at the original 31 retained daily ages. Report the first unsupported daily observation and its bracket between the preceding supported age and that age, plus an explicit horizon-sufficient status. The model does not resolve exhaustion within that one-day bracket; do not interpolate an exact deadline. This retains the original deterministic growth and quadrature without adding a new continuous-time model. The earliest binding supply sets the supported forecast prefix; preserve original potential curves/numbers beyond it but visibly mark unsupported supply and do not invent starvation physiology or clipped final yield. Numeric availability/stock margin at the chosen age remains explicit. At exact budget equality the endpoint is supported; subsequent positive consumption isn't. Zero/negative/NaN/unknown input, zero-demand paths, saturated-vapor mathematical zero and legacy missing water must be addressed deliberately.

Native supplies/needs/coverage/files views share pure calculations and protected full-precision source/definition/result/CSV exports with CLI. Loading a supply study keeps frozen imported assumptions and optional habitat observation separate from live resource stores or editable unopened paths. Show units, source/recipe residual and missing quantities before claiming sufficiency. Existing crop controls, legacy file identity, forecasts and Universe/save/golden stay unchanged.
