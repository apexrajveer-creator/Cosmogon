# Product vision

The authoritative product specification is [MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTIVE.md](MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTIVE.md),
preserved verbatim from the owner's directive on 2026-10-05. It supersedes conflicting older
high-level plans; it does not supersede the need to preserve working features or deterministic state.

Cosmogon is a native, offline-capable living-universe simulator: one authoritative world connects
astronomy, orbital changes, planetary environments, life, settlements, technology and civilization.
Its distinguishing experience is a visually compelling universe whose causes can be inspected,
understood and meaningfully edited through progressively revealed controls.

The same world supports exploration, engineering, research, education and photography. Presentation
exposure, creative lighting and cinematic audio do not silently change physical state. Scientific
values disclose whether they are observed, derived, estimated, modelled, inferred, procedural,
modified or unknown; approximations are named, not sold as validated predictions.

Complexity belongs behind intentional workspaces and advanced controls. Alerts are configurable,
never mandatory noise. The camera, control system and visual quality are core product work.

Implementation uses vertical slices. See [CURRENT_STATE_AUDIT.md](CURRENT_STATE_AUDIT.md) for verified
starting evidence and [MASTER_GAP_ANALYSIS.md](MASTER_GAP_ANALYSIS.md) for the active sequence.
The full vision is long-term work, not a claim about the current release.
