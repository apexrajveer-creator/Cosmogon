# Cumulative cabin heat accounting design

Next connected phase 9/16 build after recorded balances. Sparse observation budgets cannot establish heat flow over a day. Retain passive signed energy counters at every actual executed habitat interval, then subtract two retained ledgers. Preserve the existing temperature solver, ordering, deterministic math and physical assumptions.

NASA Glenn's [specific-heat explanation](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/specheat.html) distinguishes process-dependent heat capacity and internal energy from enthalpy. This supports careful naming; it does not calibrate our user-assumed effective structure/cabin capacity. Our operational identity is sum of actual signed modeled heat paths = sum of interval C×ΔT, with an explicit residual. Neither quantity is absolute thermodynamic energy. Changes to assumed C are counterfactual configuration edits, not physical construction or energy transfer.

New definitions explicitly opt in to `record_heat`; generated native/CLI definitions enable it. Absent legacy definitions deserialize false and retain identical physics, serialized state and replay results. No automatic adoption or invented earlier counters. Enabled habitats carry an optional model-pinned ledger from creation; disabled/legacy habitats never acquire it during stepping. World/source identity deliberately changes only for enabled habitats. Untouched-universe golden should stay unchanged; verify rather than re-pin speculatively.

Accumulate eleven signed joule paths: delivered bus electricity, conversion losses, metabolic heat, electrolysis reference enthalpy debit, CO₂-reduction heat, crew vapor phase debit, condensation heat, radiator rejection debit, signed conduction, breach temperature-equivalent cooling debit, and modeled sensible storage. Heater power is already part of delivered bus electricity. Full outflow gas enthalpy remains separately recorded and is not another debit to the lumped-temperature identity. Accumulate actual interval residuals too. Never integrate a sparse latest-minute snapshot into missing history.

Track creation coordinate, executed model seconds, unavailable-environment seconds, unsupported-start-temperature seconds and effective-capacity transitions on actual executed intervals. Missing environment and unsupported starting temperature leave heat counters unchanged and increment separate excluded coverage. A computed interval ending outside the domain is still computed; its next unsupported interval is excluded. Zero-length steps change nothing; rejected steps change nothing. Validate finite/bounded counters, coverage and definition/ledger agreement. Capacity-transition counts describe executed assumptions, not edits during skipped intervals.

Shared ordinary/all-four-paired balance reports expose actual initial/final counters, differences, coverage, capacity transitions, a summed-path residual and retained interval-residual difference. Require matching ledger origin/version and monotone coverage; report unavailable for legacy or inconsistent retained ledgers. A covered partial ledger is explicitly partial. Sum of interval C×ΔT remains meaningful model bookkeeping across capacity changes; do not replace it with endpoint C×ΔT. Native recorded Heat ledger view and protected heat.csv/report.json use the same computation and immutable export requests. Original studies remain read-only. Existing stock/counter CSV columns stay stable; the new optional heat CSV uses original SI values and full precision.

Verify actual interval accounting independently against budgets and endpoints for constant conditions, varying heat/power, optional phase/reaction/breach processes, signed conduction, configuration remainders, skipped support, old/new save roundtrip and exact replay. Preserve legacy reader/export behavior, all four independent branches and golden; test pin/tamper refusals. Required workspace tests, clippy, release app/CLI, package/signature/DMG, final captures and ordinary run precede checkpoint. No hardware calibration, physiological, uncertainty or whole thermodynamic-system closure claim.
