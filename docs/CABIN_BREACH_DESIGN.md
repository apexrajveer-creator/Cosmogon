# Finite cabin breach design

Prepared before implementation on 2026-10-09, after graph checkpoint `f78e716`. Phase 9 needs authoritative leaks/EVA; this slice implements **outward open-orifice breach flow**, not seal permeation, a suit, an airlock or certification.

Primary sources checked: [NASA Glenn mass-flow choking](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/BGP/mflchk.html), equations 10 and the M=1 reduction; [NASA GUNNS orifice theory](https://github.com/nasa/gunns/wiki/Standard_Flow_Equation), choked/subcritical equations and critical pressure ratio; [NASA Leak Correlation](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20190033458/downloads/20190033458.pdf), pages 3–7 and 10–19, distinguishes open holes from tortuous/molecular leak paths. This implementation derives the thermodynamic and numerical coupling below; it does not claim to use GUNNS or match measured hardware.

Optional strict configuration pins a separate model, throat area, assumed discharge coefficient, constant gamma, explicit external receiver pressure and minimum supported cabin pressure. Outside pressure is a design assumption, never advertised as measured site weather. Cabin O2/N2/CO2/vapor escape in their actual well-mixed mass fractions; isolated O2 reserve/H2/methane and liquids do not escape. Cumulative escaped components remain outside the cabin and are included only in a separate total accounted-mass ledger. Disabling the breach preserves history/source pins. Existing configurations/saves/golden remain byte-identical when absent.

Use Rmix=R*n/m and p=nRT/V. For r=p_out/p, rcrit=(2/(gamma+1))^(gamma/(gamma-1)), choked mdot=Cd*A*p*sqrt(gamma/(Rmix*T))*(2/(gamma+1))^((gamma+1)/(2*(gamma-1))). Above rcrit use mdot=Cd*A*p*sqrt(2*gamma/(Rmix*T*(gamma-1))*(r^(2/gamma)-r^((gamma+1)/gamma))). No inward composition/resupply is invented when receiver pressure is higher.

Operator split: retain existing crew/process/thermal interval, then integrate a finite breach substep from that actual resulting state. Treat configured lumped C as the **initial effective sensible capacity** of that substep. With cv=R/(gamma-1), infer Cs=C-n0*cv; if Cs<0 the thermal decomposition is unsupported and no breach exchange occurs. Well-mixed gas and structure share T; Cs and gamma remain fixed during the breach substep. Energy equation (Cs+n*cv)dT=R*T*dn gives T(n)=T0*((Cs+n*cv)/C)^(gamma-1). Then integrate dn/dt=-mdot/Mmix. At Cs=0 this recovers ideal adiabatic rigid-tank blowdown; large Cs approaches isothermal flow. Vapor reference phase energy leaves with escaped vapor and is removed from retained vapor, without a second sensible cooling debit.

Deterministic bounded step-doubled RK4 adapts by at most one percent of current moles per attempted step, with local step-doubling mole disagreement at most 1e-11 of initial moles and a bounded 1024-attempt workload and supported 100–500 K/pressure floor. Boundary roots use fixed bisection; never drain past equilibrium/pressure/temperature support. Record partial integrated time and explicit constraints when support/work stops. Integration-resolution tests compare tighter steps and closed-form choked cases; no universal accuracy claim. Record start/end p/T, escaped components, full outflow enthalpy, structure/gas internal-energy changes and residual. The existing constant-C thermal report additionally records a **temperature-equivalent lumped cooling debit** C*(T0-T1), not the full outflow enthalpy; its storage/residual includes that explicit split debit. This is an approximation of whole-minute coupled thermodynamics, especially during rapid blowdown, not a resolved jet, shock, condensation or variable-property model.

Native Overview/Settings/Model, ordinary undoable configuration, exact saves/model pins, protected complete numeric studies, existing graph/WHY and controlled paired settings share the same state. Acceptance requires finite proportional stocks, accounted-mass and substep-energy identities, closed/receiver/unsupported cases, no vapor double debit, deterministic save/resume/replay, old source-pinned recordings, full release tests/clippy, actual native PNG review and a normal packaged run. Field calibration, reverse flow, rarefied/seal flow, liquid entrainment, jet forces, EVA, physical greenhouse and the master directive remain incomplete.

## Inward reverse-flow extension (build 57B, added 2026-10-21)

Owner-confirmed scope for build 57 ("environmental hazard completeness") adds the breach half: opt-in reverse (inward) orifice flow. No primary source claims a validated reverse-flow leak model; the NASA choked/subcritical law is reused with the receiver as upstream, and every inward-specific choice below is an explicit authored Cosmogon assumption.

Compatibility: `MODEL` stays `cosmogon-cabin-breach/1` (bumping would refuse every existing breach save). Two `Config` fields are added with `serde(default)` + skip-when-default — `allow_inward_flow: bool` (false) and `inward_temperature_k: f64` (250 K, bounded 100–500 K in `validate`) — so legacy configurations serialize byte-identically and legacy JSON without the fields still loads. `Budget` gains `inflow: Gas` (default-skipped while zero), so outward budgets serialize unchanged.

Mechanism: when `allow_inward_flow` and `p_cabin < p_receiver`, `integrate_inward_resolution` runs the same step-doubled RK4/Budget machinery with `dn/dt = +inflow_rate/M_in`, upstream = (receiver pressure, inward temperature, R for the assumed mix), downstream = cabin. Receiver composition is **assumed dry air**: 21% O₂ / 79% N₂ by mole, zero vapor/CO₂, no reservoir depletion (`INWARD_AIR_MOLAR_KG` = 0.21·M_O₂ + 0.79·M_N₂). Thermal relation from the same constant-gamma structure+gas energy balance with imported flow enthalpy: `T(n) = (C·T₀ + Δn·cp·T_in)/(Cs + n·cv)`, `cp = cv + R`; dT/dn has constant sign `∝ cp·T_in − cv·T₀`, so at most one temperature support bound is reachable (500 K for hot inflow with γ·T_in ≥ 500; the 100 K bound is unreachable unless the cabin already sits at the floor). Energies: structure `Cs(T₁−T₀)`, gas `n₁cvT₁ − n₀cvT₀`, phase 0 (dry inflow), and `outflow_enthalpy_j = −Δn·cp·T_in` (imported enthalpy carried as negative outflow) so the reported residual closes identically to outward. `lumped_cooling_j = C(T₀−T₁)` may be negative (net heating). Rates are signed negative = inward; statuses add `InflowComplete` (supported) and `InflowTemperatureBoundary`; `Equilibrium`/`Closed`/`PressureUnsupported`/`TemperatureUnsupported`/`CapacityUnsupported`/`WorkLimit` are reused.

Habitat coupling: interval stocks apply `−escaped + inflow`; cumulative inflow lands in `Totals.inflow_gas` (skip-if-none) and `Totals.inflow_enthalpy_j` (skip-if-zero, ≥ 0 magnitude of imported enthalpy). `breach_enthalpy_j` accumulates only the positive outflow part (`max(0.0)`, identity-preserving for outward) so its ≥0 bound holds. The mass identity becomes cabin + escaped = original + cumulative inflow; the heat-ledger breach term (index 9, `−lumped_cooling`) becomes signed (inward heating legitimately accumulates positive joules) with the monotone check relaxed to match; recording closed-mass validation, the pressure-report substep gas derivation and the balance mass-block scope text include inflow. CSV columns are unchanged (110); inward evidence rides the retained budgets/totals/manifest. Diagnostics gain a `breach_inflow` term and an inward sentence in the breach explanation; report limits drop "inward" from the unmodelled-leak list; the `breach`/`habitat` registry descriptors and `breach::source()` describe the inward assumptions and non-claims.

Deliberate non-claims: receiver composition/temperature are authored, not measured; no reservoir depletion, humidity, CO₂ mixing, seal/permeation inward path, condensation on the cold inflow, or variable properties; field accuracy and uncertainty remain unquantified.
