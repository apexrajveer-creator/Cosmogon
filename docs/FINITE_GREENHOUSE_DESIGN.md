## Research and limits

Existing NASA MEC/BVAD low-light lettuce growth and optional transpiration inputs remain source-pinned. Existing 1992 crop input recipe is rounded and not conservative; the explicit separate CIAAW CHON surrogate is the material basis. NASA https://spinoff.nasa.gov/indoor-farming describes chamber air routed through lights to remove heat and control/measure humidity; https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/stmd/game-changing-development-program/next-generation-life-support-ngls/ identifies photosynthesis/transpiration as contributions to atmosphere revitalization and water recycling. These establish mechanisms, not calibration of our combined surrogate. BVAD2022 Table4-93 distinguishes fertilizer salts/element requirements, motivating explicit labeling that HNO3-equivalent is not a complete literal nutrient solution.

Photosynthesis retains chemical energy, while transpiration transfers liquid to vapor and phase enthalpy. Treating all light power as immediate sensible heat while also storing chemical/phase energy would double count. Effective biomass chemical storage requires an explicit user-assumed J/kg basis, not an inferred measured heat of formation from CHON fractions. Must independently verify total electrical/chemical/phase/sensible transfers and retain unknown physiology/hardware claims.

## Candidate integrated boundary (resolve details before Rust changes)

Optional source-pinned crop config and independently serialized state. Dedicated nutrient-equivalent and standing edible/inedible dry-mass stocks; biomass does not automatically satisfy nominal crew dry-food demand. Harvest explicitly moves held biomass to separately retained harvested stores with no energy/mass creation; full diet/calorie/fertilizer conversion remains unavailable. Initial stocks come only from original explicit setup, never from toggling equipment, later config edits or planner budgets.

Inputs draw finite existing potable water and captured CO2 after crew/previous equipment priority; product oxygen enters ordinary reserve only with capacity/headroom and becomes regulator feed next interval. Transpired water enters enabled finite vapor accounting, subject to source support and domain; no immediate condensate recovery credit. Standing/harvested biomass and nutrient state contribute accounted mass. New setup must require optional humidity accounting, all required source pins and coherent capacities; unsupported legacy water cannot be installed silently.

A separately cached bounded source curve is derived from original daily values; state pins the exact source inputs and projected curve or sufficient recomputable coefficients. Avoid repeated full monthly quadrature every minute. If using daily piecewise interpolation, expose it as an engineered controlled-process approximation, not subdaily empirical growth. Actual finite execution never simply grants the source horizon forecast after the fact.

Growth needs supported maintained environmental inputs and sufficient allocated fixture/control power, material inputs/product capacity and energy/phase feasibility. Define darkness/photoperiod and pause semantics explicitly. Unsupported operation pauses modeled progress with an explicit reason, not a fake biological survival/stress/death prediction. No growth when power or any actual feed is unavailable; delivered but insufficient fixture energy still enters an explicit thermal budget. No use of future recycled water/freed storage/new reaction products in the same interval.

Extend completed interval budgets/cumulative counters, exact heat ledger and mass diagnostics, JSON/CSV recordings/source definitions and protected replay. Enabled model pins must be retained on save/import; old recordings without crop history cannot reconstruct it. Save validation rejects impossible negative stock/age/source/conservation/pin states. Reset/disable/edits retain actual material and history; no hidden new crop cycle or resource grant.

## Selected first installed mechanism (2026-10-10, before Rust changes)

Model one finite controlled reference cycle. Explicit `assume_maintained_conditions` is a user-declared ideal chamber boundary (source 23°C, source RH/pressure/light/CO2 regime), not proof that cabin/chamber hardware maintains it. When false, no growth or lamp request. When true, real finite captured CO2, potable water and explicit nutrient-equivalent feed are consumed; generated O2 enters bounded reserve, dry biomass enters separate standing stores and vapor enters existing finite cabin humidity accounting. Dedicated initial nutrient and captured-CO2 stocks exist only in original explicit habitat setup. No literal fertilizer or validated chamber claim.

Source progress is effective supported production progress, not chronological biological age. Map a 16-hour light period in each fixed terrestrial day, advancing source progress only during fully powered light. Retain actual clock origin separately. Missing feeds/power pause progress; this does not imply survival, reversible physiological stress, or later calibrated catch-up. Use 31 original daily source cumulative values, explicitly piecewise linear between them. Largest feasible progress is found by bounded monotonic bisection over the actual interval target, checking feed/headroom and source-derived chemical plus vapor-phase energy against delivered light electricity. Auxiliary assumed power remains explicit and separate.

Pin source crop definition, reference CHON material basis and user-assumed 20 MJ/kg dry chemical storage basis (editable only before a new installed source cycle). This energy value is an engineering input, not a measured tissue heat of formation. Formula alone does not establish it. Subtract actual chemical/phase storage from bus sensible heat; retain signed optional heat ledger supplements without changing original eleven-term arrays or source catalogs in legacy studies. Store/revalidate the compact 31-row source curve to avoid quadrature every minute. Original supply/chemistry planner reports are unchanged.

Harvest is an explicit completed-cycle material transfer to distinct harvested edible/inedible dry stores. It does not feed the crew's fixed full-diet model and does not start a hidden new cycle. Disabling retains stocks, source and history. Operational maintenance assumption/auxiliary power and safe capacities may change; original crop/chemical basis cannot silently rewrite held production. New cycles, actual chamber climate/control/respiration/stress/solution chemistry and dietary conversion remain future builds.
